Ransomware encrypts your files and databases and demands payment for the key. Decryption is our first goal: we test every known decryptor for the strain and analyse its encryption for weaknesses we can use.

When no decryption path exists, the work continues. Our own tools reconstruct files and databases from the data the attack left behind, and every recovered item passes an integrity check before it is handed back.
