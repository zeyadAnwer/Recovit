Cloud services keep your data, but they also copy mistakes and attacks instantly. A hijacked account can delete mailboxes; ransomware on one laptop can encrypt the folders a whole team syncs.

We work through the provider’s version and retention layers, its audit logs and your synced devices to bring the data back, then secure every account involved.
