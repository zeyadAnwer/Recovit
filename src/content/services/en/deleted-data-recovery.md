Data disappears in two ways: by accident or on purpose. A wrong command, a sync that emptied shared folders, an employee wiping their work before leaving, an insider, or an attacker erasing data and logs to hide what they did.

We recover deleted data from file systems, mail stores, databases and cloud retention layers. When the deletion was deliberate, we also document who did it and when.
