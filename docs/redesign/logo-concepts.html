Recovik — logo concepts

Three directions for “data brought back after an incident”. Each is shown at 128, 32 and 16 px, on light and dark. Concept A is implemented across the site, favicons and OG images; B and C are kept for review.

Implemented

A — Restore R

The bowl of the R is a rewind arc whose arrowhead closes around a recovered data block: the letter, the action and the outcome in one shape.

Files: public/brand/recovik-mark*.svg, recovik-lockup-en*.svg, recovik-lockup-bilingual*.svg (light, dark, mono).

Alternative

B — Made whole

A data block with its missing corner fragment returning: the simplest “broken shape made whole”, strongest at 16 px but the least specific to Recovik.

Alternative

C — Rewind ring

A counter-clockwise restore arc around a recovered block: reads instantly as “restore”, but sits close to generic backup and sync icons.
